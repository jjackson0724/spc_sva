Serving Those Who Served on Campus

The St. Petersburg College SVA Chapter provides a supportive community, student advocacy, and essential academic resources for veterans, active duty members, and military families.

🎓 Get Started With VA Benefits Explore Resources Connect With SVA

OUR MISSION

Empowering St. Petersburg College veterans, service members, and military families through community connection, campus advocacy, and pathways to academic and professional success.

🤝 Community & Connection

Building a supportive, welcoming environment where veterans, active duty members, and military dependents connect through shared experiences, social gatherings, and campus events.

⚖️ Advocacy & Support

Serving as a unified voice for all military-connected students - ensuring smooth access to education benefits, campus veteran services, and dedicated advising.

🎓 Academic & Career Success

Equipping our members and military families with professional mentorship, leadership opportunities, and networking to thrive at SPC and beyond.

🏥 Wellness & Family Support

Campus Veterans Centers, Bay Pines VA resources, crisis support networks, and military family relief funds.
